# What Withheld cannot tell you

1. **Accuracy on hidden outcomes is unknown.** Real privacy-suppressed outcomes are unobserved. A held-out published cohort is not a substitute for them; suppression is not random.
2. **80% is a target, not the achieved overall rate.** Observed coverage is 77.554% overall and 74.632% for the smallest published programs. Grouped and heterogeneous data may violate the exchangeability assumptions behind formal conformal guarantees. We report empirical coverage only.
3. **It predicts a program median, not your salary.** Outcomes depend on individual choices, labor markets, location, completion, employment and other factors.
4. **No causal ROI.** A difference between programs does not establish the causal benefit of one college, account for ability selection, or include tuition, living costs or opportunity costs.
5. **Vintage and population mismatch.** A June 2026 release is not a June 2026 earnings measurement. Earnings and debt can describe different cohorts and reference-dollar years. The app preserves source dollars, without pretending to forecast or inflation-adjust to today.
6. **Public data is already privacy-treated.** ED describes differentially private perturbation and suppression for earnings. Published values are noisy historical statistics, not exact underlying IRS values.
7. **Completion counts are not earnings cohort sizes.** We use two IPEDS completion counts as a rough size feature and calibration band, and do not call them sample sizes of earners.
8. **Pooled outcomes.** Branches may share one institution-family outcome. Training/test outcomes are deduplicated by OPEID6, field and credential; the explorer retains each campus record for lookup. Record counts are not graduate counts.
9. **Abstention is deliberate.** Suppressed programs below 30 training peers in the same field and credential are not estimated. Other missing outcomes are not assumed to be privacy suppression and are not filled.
10. **No official federal eligibility benchmark.** The slider is an illustrative user-selected reference. It cannot determine loan eligibility or whether a program passes a regulatory test.
11. **Loan math is illustrative.** The calculator assumes fixed-rate amortization, no fees, no subsidy and no income-driven repayment. The 6.5% default is an example, not a current loan rate. Comparing debt to earnings across cohorts is not an affordability recommendation.
12. **Research only.** This is not financial advice, a regulated financial product, an automated lender, or a system operated with real money. No patients, personal financial records or identifying student records are used.
13. **Static snapshot.** School names/programs may have changed, closed or moved since the source collection. This is not a current admissions catalog. Browser comparison selections are session-local, not a durable account.
