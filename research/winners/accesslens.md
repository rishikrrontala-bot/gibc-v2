## Pitch, verbatim
> **Most accessibility tools check whether a student can access the page. AccessLens checks whether they can access the learning.**

## The wow moment
Paste a real assignment and it's decomposed into the actions a student performs, each with its cognitive, motor, sensory and timing demand. The tool flags which demands *have nothing to do with what's being graded*, then proposes fixes that keep the academic difficulty.
